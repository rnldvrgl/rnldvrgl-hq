# Sound effects: sources and licences

Every sound effect in HQ is a real recording. All of them are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain
dedication): no attribution is required, but here is where each one came from anyway. The licence was checked
on each sound's own page when it was downloaded (November 2026).

The files were cut to length, mixed to mono, level-matched, given short fades (and for the loops, a crossfaded
seam) and encoded as 96 kbps MP3. The office radio's music is not here: it is still generated live in
`lib/ambience.ts`.

Sources:
- **BigSoundBank** (bigsoundbank.com, Joseph Sardin and contributors): "CC0 (public domain): Free and royalty-free" on every page used.
- **Freesound** (freesound.org): each sound marked "Creative Commons 0" on its page (the files are Freesound's HQ previews of those sounds).
- **OpenGameArt** (opengameart.org): pack licensed CC0.
